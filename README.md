# Growth Audit — Coursology

**Live:** https://hribeiro-adapty.github.io/coursology-flow-rebuild/

Five ranked ideas for the Coursology: Learn Anything onboarding and paywall, plus a clickable prototype rebuilt in Coursology's own design. Only the sequence changes. The art direction stays yours.

| # | Idea | Potential outcome |
|---|------|-------------------|
| 01 | Show their first episode before the price | CR +10–20% |
| 02 | Put their topic in the paywall headline | CR +15–20% |
| 03 | Offer the year when they close the paywall | ARPU +10–15% |
| 04 | Give "For school" its own path | CR +5–15% |
| 05 | Bring the student proof to the paywall | CR +5–10% |

Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps, not measured lift for Coursology. Every price, rating, quote and feature line comes from Coursology's own App Store listing, website, pricing page and in-app screens.
